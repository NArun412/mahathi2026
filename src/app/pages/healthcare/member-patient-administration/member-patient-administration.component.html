Member / Patient Administration

Mahathi Infotech enables seamless member and patient administration by supporting enrollment, digital engagement, and customer service operations across healthcare organizations.

Eligibility & Enrollment Support

We support enrollment workflows across Individual, Group, Employer, Medicare, and Medicaid programs. Our teams manage eligibility processing, life-event changes, plan updates, and downstream system synchronization to ensure accuracy and compliance.

Member / Patient Portals – Custom Development

Mahathi designs and builds secure, scalable portals that allow members and patients to:

  • View ID cards, benefits, and coverage details
  • Submit and track prior authorizations and referrals
  • Communicate securely with service teams

These portals reduce call volume while improving experience and transparency.

Customer Service Enablement
